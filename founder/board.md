## 2. Cross-Board Identified Risks (Raised by Multiple Members)

1. **Edge-Case Wake-Up Failure Is Fatal to Brand Trust**:
   Both the Offers and Product lenses warned that a destination alarm app has zero margin for error. If a commuter misses their stop even once—due to an unmapped subway tunnel, Bluetooth earphone disconnect, or operating system battery killing the background process—user trust is destroyed permanently.
2. **Distribution & ARPU Economics at ₹26/Month**:
   Both the Offers and Monopoly lenses stressed that at ₹26/month (~$0.31 USD), paid customer acquisition (Facebook/Google Ads) is economically unviable. The product must possess zero-CAC organic distribution loops (such as station QR codes or peer-to-peer transit sharing) to achieve sustainable growth.
3. **Big Tech Platform Threat & Copycats**:
   The Monopoly lens emphasized that Google Maps or Apple Maps could theoretically replicate a basic distance alarm. TransitWake's defense must reside in its 10x battery efficiency (~78% savings via dynamic GPS tiers), subterranean offline tile caching, privacy-first zero tracking, and deep focus on the commuter sleep ritual.
