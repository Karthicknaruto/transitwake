# TransitWake

Never miss your train, metro, or bus stop again. Sleep or read in total peace.

TransitWake sounds a high-intensity Cockpit Dark alarm and speaks station callouts into your earphones 4.0 km before your destination.

- **Saves your battery**: Dynamic GPS polling tiers use up to ~78% less battery than standard navigation apps, so your phone won't die on the ride home.
- **Works in subway tunnels**: Subterranean offline caching keeps map tiles and distance calculations running even in cellular deadzones underground.
- **Safety first**: Zero popup ads during alarms. When you wake up, a single tap dismisses or snoozes the siren.
- **Pricing**: The core alarm is 100% free forever. Upgrade to TransitWake Prime for ₹26/month (about $0.31) to completely remove all advertisements. 14-day money-back guarantee, cancel anytime with one tap.
